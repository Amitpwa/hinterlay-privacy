# Hinterlay — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_ · Publisher: [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] · Contact: [EMAIL]

**Your photos stay on your device.** Hinterlay edits photos you choose through Android's photo
picker. Subject detection runs on your phone using Google ML Kit. We do not upload, store or
share your photos. Exported images are saved to your gallery (Pictures/Hinterlay) and shared
only when you choose to share them.

**Ads.** Hinterlay is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your
device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address), app interaction data and
diagnostic information to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and
Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, we ask for your choices through Google's consent
tool, and you can change them any time from the app menu → *Privacy & ad choices*.
Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

**What we collect ourselves:** nothing. Hinterlay has no account, no analytics of its own and
no server.

**Children.** Hinterlay is not directed at children under 13.

**Changes.** We'll update this page and the date above if anything changes.
