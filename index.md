# Hinterlay Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 8 October 2026
**Publisher:** Ashutosh Pandey
**Contact:** [ashutosh.pandey@amzbizsol.in](mailto:ashutosh.pandey@amzbizsol.in)

Hinterlay is a photo editor for Android that puts text behind the subject of your photo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your photos stay on your phone.**

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Your photos** | Processed on your device. Never uploaded, never sent to us. |
| **Accounts** | None. You do not sign up or log in. |
| **What we receive** | Anonymous usage counts only (for example "an export happened"). You can switch this off. |
| **Ads** | Shown by Google AdMob. You choose your ad consent. |
| **Payments** | Handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. |
| **Children** | Not directed at children under 13. |

---

## 1. Your photos stay on your device

Hinterlay edits photos you choose with Android's photo picker, or take with the in-app camera. Subject detection, text placement, erasing, cropping and skin smoothing all run **on your phone** using Google ML Kit. We do not upload, store on a server, or share your photos. Exported images are saved to your gallery (Pictures/Hinterlay) and are shared only when you choose to share them.

## 2. Camera and location

- **Camera.** Used only while the camera screen is open, to take a photo you choose to edit. Face masks are placed with on-device face detection. Faces are not stored, identified or uploaded.
- **Approximate location.** Used only if you tap the **Place** stamp, to print the name of your city or area on a photo. Your coordinates are turned into a place name by Android's built-in geocoder (which may use Google's location service). We do not save or receive your location.

Both permissions are optional. The app works without them.

## 3. Saved creations

Your history (edited photos, text settings and previews) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Delete any item from **Library**, or clear the app's data, to remove it. Nothing is synced to us.

## 4. Ads

Hinterlay is free and shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google may use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), app interaction data and diagnostic information to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud.

- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, you are asked for your choices through Google's consent tool before ads load.
- You can change those choices any time in **More, Ad choices**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android **Settings, Privacy, Ads**.

Learn more: [How Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 5. Usage data and analytics

To understand which features are useful, Hinterlay uses:

| Service | What it records | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Firebase and Google Analytics** | A random install identifier, app version, Android version, language, which screens are opened, and counts of actions such as exports or stickers made. | No photos, typed text, names, emails or phone numbers. |
| **Microsoft Clarity** | How menus and browsing screens are used (taps, scrolls, screen layout). | **Switched off on every screen that can show your photos:** the editor, crop and camera. |

Both follow one switch: **More, Share anonymous usage data**. Turn it off and they stop. You can also decline when the app first asks.

## 6. Notifications and content

If you allow notifications, your device receives occasional tips and updates through Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn this off in **More, Tips and updates**, or in Android settings. Inspiration images and announcements shown in the app are downloaded from our server.

## 7. On-device AI

Caption ideas, font suggestions and colours ("Do magic") are generated **on your phone** from the photo you open. The photo never leaves your device.

## 8. Purchases and support

Tips and the lifetime ad-free option are processed by **Google Play**. We only receive confirmation that a purchase happened, so we can remove ads for supporters. If a PayPal donation link is shown, it opens PayPal in your browser; PayPal handles that payment under its own policy, we receive no payment details from it, and a donation unlocks nothing in the app.

## 9. Sharing and invites

When you share a creation, the share sheet is handled by Android and the app you pick. Your invite code is a random code stored on your device. It does not identify you.

## 10. Who receives data

We do not sell your data. The only third parties that receive information are the services above: Google (AdMob, Firebase, Analytics, Play), Microsoft (Clarity), and PayPal if you use the optional donate link. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

## 11. Your choices and rights

- Turn off usage data: **More, Share anonymous usage data**.
- Change ad consent: **More, Ad choices**.
- Delete everything stored by the app: uninstall it, or clear its storage in Android settings.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold only anonymous data, we usually cannot link it to you, but you are welcome to ask: write to the contact address above.

## 12. Data retention and security

Anonymous usage data is kept for as long as the analytics providers' standard retention periods allow, then deleted. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Photos and creations never leave your device, so they are protected by your phone's own security.

## 13. Children

Hinterlay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top and ask you to accept it again in the app.
